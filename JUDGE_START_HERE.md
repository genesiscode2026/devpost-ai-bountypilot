## 1. What Is BountyPilot AI?
An autonomous diligence and proof-of-work agent for Web3 and AI developers. It continuously scans decentralized bounty platforms (WorkUSDC, Superteam), enforces a deterministic 6-gate policy compliance filter (PC0 to PC5) requiring genuine escrow and Base USDC settlement, and generates tamper-proof SHA-256 Proof Packets to prevent work disputes.
